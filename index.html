About Us

Tax and compliance, handled properly

Who are we?

B & D Associates is a tax consultancy and financial advisory practice based in Bhayander (East), Thane. We look after the tax, accounting and statutory compliance needs of individuals and businesses, so that our clients can concentrate on running their business.

What do we do?

We prepare and file returns, maintain and reconcile books of account, keep track of statutory due dates, and assist with notices, assessments and registrations under Income Tax, GST and corporate laws.

Whom do we serve?

Salaried individuals, professionals, traders, manufacturers, service providers, partnership firms, LLPs and private limited companies.

Areas of Practice

  • Income Tax & TDS compliance
  • GST returns, reconciliation & registrations
  • Accounting, bookkeeping & Tally data management
  • Company & LLP incorporation and ROC compliance support
  • Replies to Income Tax and GST notices
  • Business structuring & financial advisory
  • Business registrations and licences

Documents We Usually Need

  1. PAN, Aadhaar and income tax portal login
  2. Form 16 (salaried) / Form 16A and other TDS certificates
  3. Bank statements and interest certificates for the full year
  4. Capital gains statements from brokers / mutual funds; property sale and purchase documents
  5. Proofs for deductions claimed (where applicable)
  6. Books of account / sales and purchase summary (for business income)
  7. Bank account details for refund

We also review your AIS and Form 26AS before filing.
